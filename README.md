# Pairidaeza

A workspace for your bots and conversations.

Give each project its own space, keep related chats together, and create bots
with instructions that fit the work. Start with a bot template or make your own.
Pairidaeza has interfaces for desktop, mobile, and the browser.

## Explore the preview

Pairidaeza is in early development. Account sign-in is real, but application
content currently uses demo data. This preview lets you explore the workspace;
it is not a production AI service.

You can:

- Create bots with their own names, colours, and instructions.
- Start with reusable bot templates instead of a blank prompt.
- Organise bots and group chats into private spaces.
- Find chats, bot settings, and pages with search.

## Get started

Packaged releases are not published yet. You need access to an existing
Pairidaeza app or browser instance to follow these steps.

1. Open Pairidaeza and choose **Sign in**.
2. Complete sign-in in your browser.
3. Open an existing chat to explore the demo workspace.
4. To make a bot, choose **New bot** from **Create**.
5. Enter a name and choose a colour.
6. Choose **Create** to open the bot's chat.

## Start with a template

Open **Integrations** and choose **Templates**. Browse or search for a template,
then open it to read its instructions. Choose **Create bot** to add the bot and
open its chat.

## Keep projects separate

Choose **New private space** from **Create**. Name the space and choose
**Create space**. Each space has its own bots and group chats.

Use **Search** to return to a chat, find bot settings, or open a page. On desktop,
press **Command+K** on macOS or **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux to open search.
